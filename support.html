Common questions

Where is Doppio? It's a menu bar app with no Dock icon. Look for the coffee cup near the clock. If your menu bar is crowded, macOS may tuck it away — hold ⌘ and drag it to the right.

How do I open the menu? Right-click (or Control-click) the cup. A normal click turns Doppio on or off.

How do I keep awake until a Terminal command finishes? Run
open -g "doppio://wait?label=Job" && your-command; open -g "doppio://done?label=Job"

All Terminal and script commands

Doppio understands these doppio:// links. Run them with open -g "…" (keep the quotes). The same list is in the app: right-click the cup › Until a Task Finishes › From Terminal or Scripts…

  • doppio://start — turn on (add ?minutes=30 for a timer)
  • doppio://stop — turn off
  • doppio://toggle — turn on or off
  • doppio://wait?label=NAME — stay on until the matching done
  • doppio://wait?label=NAME&pid=1234 — stay on until process 1234 quits
  • doppio://done?label=NAME — that task has finished; add &notify=0 for no notification

A task started this way never turns off a session you started yourself.

AI coding tools such as Claude Code can call these from hooks so your Mac stays awake while they work: doppio://wait?label=Claude when a prompt is sent, doppio://done?label=Claude&notify=0 when it finishes.

Does Doppio change my settings? No. It uses Apple's standard power management while it's on. Quit Doppio and everything is as before.

Why does it ask for calendar access? Only if you turn on "During Calendar Events". Your events are read on your Mac and never leave it.
